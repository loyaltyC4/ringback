Straight answers before you switch it on.

Will callers know it is AI?
Most don't ask. It answers with your business name, speaks in plain Australian English and doesn't pretend to be a specific person. If someone asks directly, it tells them it's the automated booking line and offers to put them through to you.
What happens when it can't help?
It stops trying. Anything outside its rules gets a warm handoff to your mobile with the caller's name, number and problem already captured, or a callback booked in if you're unreachable.
Can it promise a price?
No, and that's deliberate. It's locked out of quoting, discounting and committing to scope. It gathers the job details and books the visit. You keep every pricing conversation.
Does it work with my job software?
Jobs push into ServiceM8, Tradify and simPRO. If you run something else, tell me on the setup call and I'll confirm before you pay anything.
How quickly can we launch?
Inside 48 hours. Setup call, I build the flow for your trade and suburb, you call it and pick it apart, then we point your number at it.
What if I hate it?
Cancel and we unpoint the number the same day. No lock-in contract, no exit fee. You were a founding customer, not a hostage.
